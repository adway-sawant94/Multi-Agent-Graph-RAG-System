Federal Privacy Regulation - Act Z (Data Sharing Restrictions)
This act governs the transfer of protected customer data containing Personally Identifiable Information (PII) by domestic corporations.

Section 102 (Cross-Border Compliance):
All domestic corporations must comply with Federal Privacy Regulations. Section 102 strictly prohibits the sharing of protected Customer Data (including PII) with any Foreign Entities or storing such data in a Foreign Jurisdiction that lacks an established data adequacy treaty with the federal government. Foreign jurisdictions that lack adequate safeguards or allow arbitrary local government access are designated as High-Risk Jurisdictions. Any domestic entity found sharing PII with organizations in these high-risk areas is subject to immediate regulatory fines and cessation of data transfers.

Section 103 (Security Mandates):
All databases containing PII must mandate multi-factor authentication and end-to-end encryption.
